WHY THE ILR IS PRIMARY
  CLR coordinates of independent data correlate at -0.02325 on average,
  against the -1/(D-1) = -0.02326 the singularity predicts. The artefact is
  harmless only while D is the same for every survey, which is why D is
  held at 44 here and stated rather than assumed.

PREREQUISITES FOR LOG-RATIO WORK
